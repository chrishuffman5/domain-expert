## Capabilities and context

- Capabilities: text, image, and video understanding; agentic reasoning; coding and cowork; adaptive/enabled/disabled reasoning modes.
- Context/output: 1M tokens; approximately 428B total and 23B activated parameters in official repository.
- Strengths: Native multimodal and million-token context; Reasoning can be enabled, adaptive, or disabled; Open-weight distribution and hosted access both available.
- Limitations: Full model is large and expensive to self-host; Official performance/speed comparisons are company-authored; Do not infer weight license from availability or repository source-code license.
